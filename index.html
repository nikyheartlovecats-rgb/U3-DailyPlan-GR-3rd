Leader in Me Habit Integrated

Topic: Some and Any (Our World 3 - Unit 3)

CNB Competency & Learning Achievement: Applies grammatical structures correctly in oral and written English. Achievement: Construct accurate sentences using "some" and "any" regarding food quantities.

Key Competencies & Montano Habit: Critical thinking, teamwork, effective communication. Habit 3: "Put First Things First" (Prioritizing tasks and grammar study).

Moment I: Introduction Motivante & Vocabulary Review (~5 mins)

Activating Prior Knowledge

Warm-Up Activity: Vocabulary 1 & 2 Quick Flash / Song Chant

Review Vocabulary 1 (food items like *a bottle of oil, a box of cereal, a bowl of sugar, a bag of rice, a jar of olives*) and Vocabulary 2 (*money, price, compare, buy, put away*). Sing song *“Let’s Go Shopping!”* (SB p. 98) with partner pointing to pictures.

Leader in Me Connection (Habit 1 - Be Proactive): Students take initiative to participate actively in pronunciation chants and recall grocery items confidently.
🏠 Take-Home Research Challenge: Ask a family member what healthy food item they always buy at the local market and write down its name in English!

Moment II: Trabajo Individual (20 mins)

Independent Production

Students complete Student Book exercises and Workbook pages independently, avoiding copy-paste by writing original sentences based on personal preferences.

Expected Answers: 1. We have *some* milk in the fridge. 2. Do we have *any* apples? No, we don't have *any*.

Self-Check: Students check their own grammar spelling and punctuation using a green colored pencil.

🏠 Take-Home Research Challenge: Look inside your kitchen pantry at home and list 3 items using "some" (e.g., *some rice*) and 2 items you don't have using "any" (e.g., *any cookies*).

Moment III: Trabajo Cooperativo / en Equipo (30 mins)

Roles & Product Creation
Role 1: Leader (Synergizer)

Keeps the team focused and ensures everyone participates equally (Habit 6 - Synergize).

Role 2: Writer / Secretary

Records team sentences and mini-dialogues on the cooperative mini-poster.

Role 3: Presenter

Shares the team's creative grocery list or roleplay during plenary.

Team Task: Create a "Supermarket Shopping Cart Menu" mini-poster combining target grammar structures with correct pricing and quantities.

🏠 Take-Home Research Challenge: Discuss with your parents how much a bag of rice or milk costs at your neighborhood supermarket and bring the price to class tomorrow.

Moment IV: Plenaria & Interactive Game (15 mins)

Interactive Closure

Teams present their mini-posters in a 2-minute showcase explaining their shopping list sentences.

🎮 Interactive Grammar Trivia Challenge (Classroom Game)

Question 1: Do we have _____ bananas in the kitchen?

🏠 Take-Home Research Challenge: Teach a family member one new grammar rule or vocabulary word you learned today before dinner!

Plenary Group Evaluation Checklist & Rubric

Teacher Nicole De León uses this interactive rubric during Moment IV group presentations.

Evaluation Criteria Excellent (4 pts) Satisfactory (3 pts) In Progress (2 pts) Score
1. Domain of Topic & Clarity Explains target grammar effortlessly with clear pronunciation. Explains clearly with minor prompts. Needs significant guidance.
2. Logical Procedure Sentences and examples follow perfect grammatical structure. Mostly correct sentence formation. Frequent grammatical errors.
3. Collaborative Work (Synergy) All team members participate equitably (Habit 6). Most members participate. Only one person dominates.
4. Q&A Response Capacity Answers peer and teacher questions accurately. Answers with team support. Struggles to answer questions.
